A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Before we get started.

Can I buy and download a project immediately?

The catalogue currently shows topics available on request. Contact us to confirm a working demo, deliverables, price and timeline before paying. Instant checkout and downloads will come when completed packages are ready.

Can I change the topic or technology stack?

Yes. Send the brief and school requirements so we can agree what needs to change and provide a quote.

Will I be able to understand the code?

Code explanations and a project walkthrough can be included in your agreed support package. The aim is to help you adapt the project and explain how it works.

Does an IoT package include hardware?

Hardware requirements and costs are confirmed separately. Tell us what components you already have.

How should I use a project kit?

Use it as a learning foundation, adapt it to your own work, acknowledge reused materials and follow your school’s project rules.
