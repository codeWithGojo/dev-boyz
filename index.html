Project FAQs

What do I need to get started?

A rough idea is enough. Share any examples, existing site links, or deadlines you have.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. Send us the link and explain what you’d like to change. We’ll review the site and agree on the scope.

How much will it cost?

Website and software projects start at ₦150,000. Your final quote depends on the work and support you need.

Who owns the code and domain?

We agree who owns the source code, domain and hosting accounts in writing before you pay.

What does hosting cost, and can I pay in instalments?

Ask us to itemise hosting and domain renewals and discuss a payment schedule in your quote.

What happens after launch?

We can agree on maintenance, updates, and ongoing support as part of the project or separately.

What do you need built?

Tell us what you need, who will use it, and when you’d like it ready.
