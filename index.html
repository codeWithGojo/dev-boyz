Project FAQs

What do I need to get started?

A rough idea is enough. Share any examples, existing site links, or deadlines you have.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. Send us the link and explain what you’d like to change. We’ll review the site and agree on the scope.

How much will it cost?

We quote based on the features, complexity, content, and support you need. You’ll know the price and deliverables before work begins.

Will it work on phones?

We design for smaller screens and test the layout and touch controls before handover.

What happens after launch?

We can agree on maintenance, updates, and ongoing support as part of the project or separately.
