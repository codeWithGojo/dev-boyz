BEFORE YOU SEND THE BRIEF

A few useful answers.

What do I need to get started?

A rough idea is enough for the first conversation. If you have a brief, examples, an existing website or a deadline, include them.

Can you improve an existing website?

Yes. Share the link and explain what needs to change. We’ll review the current setup and agree a suitable scope.

How much will my project cost?

We quote based on the features, complexity, content and support required. We agree the price and deliverables before starting.

Will the website work on phones?

We design for small screens, readable content and touch controls, and check the layouts as part of delivery.

Can you help after launch?

Maintenance, updates and ongoing support can be agreed as part of the project or as a separate arrangement.
